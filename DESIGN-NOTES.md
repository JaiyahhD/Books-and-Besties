# Bestie University — Campus homepage design

This is the first coded design milestone, based on the approved campus visual.

- Open index.html with Live Server to preview.
- The homepage uses cropped artwork from the approved visual as illustrative campus scenery; its buttons, cards, navigation, and dynamic content are actual HTML.
- The other pages, authentication flows, Supabase schema, and Netlify functions are retained from the prior project. They have not been fully tested or deployed.
- The campus homepage has responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts.
- Themes, featured book and announcement use existing site settings.
- Residence hall names use live team names when configured; the three October names are display-only placeholders otherwise. No fabricated page totals are shown.
- The admin area remains private; the Campus HQ directory tile routes to admin login.
- Campus Housing is the Monthly Crown team area, not general student housing.
- Set the real Facebook URL in js/config.js or site settings.

This is a design milestone, not a claim of production-ready voting or scoring.
