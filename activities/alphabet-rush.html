🤎 THE BARISTA'S RULEBOOK

How to Play ♡

  1. Choose an available letter from A to Z.
  2. Enter a book title beginning with that letter.
  3. Add the author and your Bestie display name.
  4. Submit to complete that letter.
  5. Once completed, the letter is locked.
  6. Work together until the entire alphabet is complete!
🤎 One letter. One contribution. Every completed letter celebrates the Bestie who contributed it!

These are preview rules. Campus HQ can establish final eligibility and verification rules before November's official challenge.
