How to Play ♡

  1. Tap any square to see its reading mission.
  2. Mark a square once you've completed its task.
  3. Complete five squares in a row, column, or diagonal for Bingo!
  4. Keep going for more lines or a full blackout.

Demo markings are self-reported. Future official events may have different rules, verification requirements, and rewards.
